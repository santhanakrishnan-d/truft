Why the house stays sharp — and what happens if it does not

It remembers, on purpose Week 38 should not forget week 12. A rumour you marked false stays gone. Without that, last year’s island-stone mistake is paid for again.
It uses your names Project, lot, homeowner, trade, invoice, variation, defect, step. Without that, next year’s enquiry needs a rebuild of the house file.
It is scored before it leaves A letter at 0.78 waits. 0.92 may send. Overnight it can pull a handover date that was not in the programme. Without a pass mark, a fluent wrong sentence still goes out.
The miss is kept. The writer can change. A held letter raises the bar for the next one. You can swap a careful writer for a faster one later; the pass mark does not move. Without that, December’s chatbot is still March’s — and you keep paying the expensive writer to hide it.
