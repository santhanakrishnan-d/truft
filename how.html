Reach people where they already are

One Truft bot. Many places.

The same bot powers the website chat, WhatsApp, email, and SMS. It is not two companies, and it is not three different memories. Who is writing decides what they can see.

Before they are a customer

Ask Truft on the website

Anyone can ask. The bot explains how Truft builds, books a time, arranges a call back, or takes a name and number. Follow-up is Truft: a person, or this bot speaking as Truft.

After they sign in

The same bot, on their job

Once they are building with you, they get a login. Progress is tracked. Spend can be approved. WhatsApp and email work the same way. Anything they confirm or ask, in any of those places, is recorded in the portal. If it is not in the portal, it did not happen.

WhatsApp is not a fake window on the website. On a phone it opens the real app. On a computer it opens WhatsApp Desktop or WhatsApp Web. This mock is not wired to a live WhatsApp Business account yet, so it will not open a personal number. When it is live, the button opens Truft’s WhatsApp.

How to think in work you can check

Evals are tiny yes-or-no checks.

An eval is a micro success test. A micro definition of done. A checkable yes or no. That is the engine. Not more words. Better checks.

You already think this way on site. Waterproofing is booked Thursday 10:00, or it is not. The $4,200 stone repair is signed, or it is not. The brass taps match variation 12, or they do not. Write those checks down. The bot has to pass them before anything leaves.

A pile of words

The machine writes a long note. You pay for the words. A fluent wrong sentence still goes out. Next month you buy a bigger model so it can talk more. The job does not get clearer. The bill does.

A pass mark

Before a note, a comment, or a WhatsApp leaves, it is scored against Truft’s own checks: sounds like you, sticks to facts, does not invent a date, does not spend. Below the mark, it waits on you. A held note teaches the next one.

Turn your constraints into evals. “Never invent a price per square metre.” “Unsigned spend does not move.” “Henson Way cannot see Mustang Ave.” “A public Instagram reply may offer a visit. It may not open a live job.” Better evals make a better agent. Skills then improve until they beat that bar.

Idle software is not billed as work. When a step runs, a WhatsApp is answered, or a follow-up is drafted, those minutes are written on a calendar you can see. That is the timesheet.

How you train it

A skill is your process, written down.

You would not hire a junior and say “be helpful.” You would walk them through a variation: quoted this, actual that, because the finish changed on site, and never invent a date that is not in the programme. You would tell them what not to say on Instagram. You would tell them that unsigned spend waits.

That packet is a skill. It is an SOP. A process you already have, turned into something the bot can run. It improves the more it is used. A held note teaches the next one. A rumour you mark false stays gone.

Skills run in named time blocks. You see them on a calendar. That is accountability, not a black box.

The first two skills that must sound like you are usually these: explain a variation, and reply on Instagram. Everything else waits until those two would make you proud.

Why write it down instead of just chatting with a model?

A chat is a conversation. It forgets. A skill is a job with a name, a pass mark, and a record. When the junior is good, you keep the training. When a note fails, that failure teaches the next one. When a cheaper, faster writer appears next year, you can move the same skill onto it, if it still passes the bar. Chat does not give you that. Training does.

Why Workweaver

The agent should get more valuable the more it works.

You pick the writer. Everyday, careful, or fast. A cheaper writer that fails the eval does not go live. You are not locked to one vendor’s meter. You are locked to Truft’s standard.

Workweaver connects the tools you already have. BuildPass. Xero. WhatsApp. Email. Calendar. The website. The office application. New tools later, without starting again.

The map of how Truft works is the grammar of the business: cost-plus, the 28 steps, what you will never say, which spend waits. Feed that in. Edit every line. The bots cannot cross it. The richer the map, the less you repeat yourself.

When a problem is fixed for one customer, that fix helps every customer. Workweaver stays on the front of the AI wave so you do not have to chase tools yourself.

Henson Way is not Mustang Ave

Job facts stay in your tools.

BuildPass stays the record for photos, defects, drawings, and the programme. Xero stays the record for invoices, margin, and contingency. The bot looks those up for that job. It does not keep Priya’s invoice in a search pile that can mix with another customer.

WorkMemory is not the system of record. It holds how Truft works: voice, evals, skills, standing rules. Pointers, not the books. If job cost and progress lived in a mixed search pile, Henson Way could bleed into Mustang Ave. That is not good enough for a builder. So the bot asks your tools, per job, and stops if the lookup is not clean.

Where it lives, and who can look

On your infrastructure. Not in my account.

The customer portal and the office application sit with truft.com.au, on machines you control. Your own data centre, or any cloud you choose. I customise and integrate. Names, photos, invoices, and WhatsApp stay yours.

Simple version of zero trust: I do not get a standing key to your customer files. If something is badly broken and a person at Bit Foundry genuinely needs to look, you open a ticket. We name who looks, why, and for how long. You can sit on the call. You can say no. That look is logged. There is no quiet back door.

The controls are built so you can go for SOC 2 and ISO 27001. Eligible, not certified. I will not pretend a certificate we do not have.

I do not collect your customer data without permission. Usage is counted so we can bill fairly. A ticket is only for troubleshooting, with your say-so.

The office phone, spoken plainly

02 7235 0833 can stay as the public number. If you want the same number to answer as a computer, Australian 02 landlines can be moved for voice. That takes paperwork and can take weeks. SMS does not run on an 02 landline. SMS uses a mobile number. If you do not want to move 02, we keep it as it is and add a new number only for the parts a computer must answer. I will not promise a live phone product that is not in place yet.

Spoken plainly

Six months to design. Then we build.

The first six months are not “switch it on tomorrow.” They are design, refine, and scope. We sit with how Truft already works. We write the first evals and skills. We prove them on a real job. Closer to the end of that window, we are ready to roll out.

What you would set up, and why

Your tools. I connect them.

None of this replaces the site file or the ledger. The office application and the customer portal read them. You should not need to live in BuildPass or Xero for the daily view. You still keep paying for the tools you already trust.

WhatsApp, in the company name

Homeowners already write there. A fake chat window on a website is not WhatsApp. You start a WhatsApp Business account as Truft. The name people see is Truft. It can take a few days, and it has to be done in the company’s own Meta Business. I cannot finish that from Coimbatore on your behalf. Keep 02 7235 0833 on the card.

The site file stays in BuildPass

Photos, defects, the programme, and the handset on site already live there. Copying them into a second system is how facts drift. On a Pro plan you switch on a machine door (Oceania). Published today: projects, photos, defects, meetings, timesheets. If a drawing field is not on that list yet, we use what is published and ask them. We do not invent it.

The ledger stays in Xero

Invoices, margin, and contingency already live there. Spend on the portal is a view of those lines, not a second set of books. You click Allow on the SS Developers organisation. Unsigned spend cannot move until someone allows it. Homeowners never receive a Xero login.

Google and Microsoft as the login

Most owners already have one of those. After they are building, that is how they sign in to their job. Apple and Facebook can wait. Homeowners must not need Facebook to see their project.
